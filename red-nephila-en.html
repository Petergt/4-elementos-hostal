OTHER WAYS TO TRAVEL

Our vision

We support local producers and traders, preserve ancestral knowledge and respect the land and the people who call it home. We seek tourism that does not gentrify and makes genuine exchanges possible.

Connect through shared values

The 4 Elementos network brings together people who want to travel in line with their values. A space to connect with other travellers, share different ways of life and take part in a circular and ethical economy.

EXPERIENCIA 4 ELEMENTOS

Your role as a contributor

Spread the word about the 4 Elementos Experience and invite other travellers to join the Daytrips. You receive a commission for each participant you bring.

Benefits of collaborating

  • Join a collaborative network for a circular and ethical economy.
  • Receive support from the 4 Elementos network.
  • Earn income for the participants you invite to the Daytrips.

The programme also offers possible extra commissions and discounts at network establishments when expectations are exceeded. Ask the team about current terms and benefits.
